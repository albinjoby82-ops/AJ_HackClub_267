Reading and video

Forty years of people have documented how they did it, and all of it is free. Start with the video.

01 Watch
Video · Veritasium The Fastest Maze-Solving Competition On Earth Watch this one first. Search runs, speed runs and why top mice look the way they do. Video · UKMARS A full contest run, start to finish What an entire slot looks like: explore, return, speed run, escalate. Video · IEEE Bruins Micromouse lecture: flood fill A whiteboard-level university lecture, exactly the depth you need. Video · APEC 2024 HAL900's fastest run yet Top-tier diagonal running. Watch the route it chooses, not just the speed.
02 Rules and books

Overview · Wikipedia Micromouse A fast orientation to the sport, its variants and its records.
