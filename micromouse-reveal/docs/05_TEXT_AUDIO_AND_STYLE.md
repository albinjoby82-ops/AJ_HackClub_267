## Music direction

Original or royalty-free high-energy electronic track:
- 125-140 BPM
- industrial percussion
- crisp transient hits
- warm low-end
- tension break at 0:24
- escalating motor-like synth after 0:27
- clean final impact

Avoid:
- copyrighted commercial tracks
- generic corporate future-bass
- overly aggressive dubstep
- comedic meme sound effects

## Colour treatment

Historical images:
- unify with a subtle cool teal grade
- preserve skin tones
- lift orange/red accents selectively
- add only light grain

Maze:
- matte deep teal
- mint sensor light
- orange route line
- neutral realistic shadows

Final:
- supplied ElecSoc logo colours remain authoritative

## Type motion

Impact words:
- fast mask-on
- 102-108% scale overshoot
- settle in 4-6 frames
- subtle tracking contraction

Statistics:
- numbers land first
- labels land 2-3 frames later
- one clean bass hit per statistic

Final title:
- title lands toward camera
- no spinning
- no perspective distortion that harms readability
- hold at least 2.2 seconds
