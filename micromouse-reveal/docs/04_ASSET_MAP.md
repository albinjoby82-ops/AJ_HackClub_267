## Image handling rules

- Never use image fill modes that stretch the source.
- Preserve face proportions.
- Use `object-fit: cover` only after manually choosing safe focal positions.
- Use subtle grain and colour unification rather than heavy filters.
- Do not place two almost identical crowd images in the same shot.
- Do not show a designed card for longer than approximately 0.6 seconds unless it is the final title.
- Keep text already embedded in designed cards readable or crop it out deliberately; never leave half-visible words.
