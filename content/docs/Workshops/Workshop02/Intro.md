# Introduction

Motors turn electrical signals into movement. In this workshop, you will begin with a small servo because it is easy to simulate and can be controlled directly from an Arduino signal pin.

You will learn:

- How PWM represents a control value
- How a servo moves to a requested angle
- How a potentiometer becomes a physical input
- Why larger motors require drivers and separate power
- The meaning of torque, back-EMF and H-bridges

Use [the at-home setup](AtHome.md) to choose Tinkercad or a basic physical kit, then follow the [workshop route](LabStructure.md).
