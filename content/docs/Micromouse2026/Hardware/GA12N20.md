# H6 - N20 Motors and Motor A Encoder

The supplied tests drive the left motor through Motor A and the right motor through Motor B. The separate encoder test checks Motor A on GPIO21 and GPIO22.

![Motor control and Motor A encoder wiring](../../assets/images/micromouse-motor-wiring.svg)

## Motor connections

| Connection | Event wiring |
|---|---|
| Left motor power wires M1/M2 | Driver Motor A outputs |
| Right motor power wires | Driver Motor B outputs |
| Driver DIR1 / PWM1 | GPIO0 / GPIO2 |
| Driver DIR2 / PWM2 | GPIO3 / GPIO10 |
| Driver VM | 9 V motor battery positive |
| Battery negative and driver GND | ESP32 GND, all joined |
| Driver VCC, only if present | ESP32 3V3 |

Use USB to power the controller. Raise the wheels: [step 7](#/docs/Micromouse2026/Debug/07_motor_1.md) and [step 8](#/docs/Micromouse2026/Debug/08_motors_2.md) start automatically. The sketches run at PWM 140 and cap duty at **170/255** for the 9 V supply and 6 V N20 motors. Keep this limit; PWM is not a regulated 6 V supply. Switch off before swapping a motor’s two power wires to correct its direction.

## Motor A encoder wiring

| Encoder pin | ESP32-C6 |
|---|---|
| C1 | GPIO21 |
| C2 | GPIO22 |
| VCC | 3V3, never the motor battery |
| GND | GND |

Use the printed pin labels; the source does not define wire colours or physical connector order. Encoder wires are not required for steps 7 and 8. The supplied encoder sketch tests **Motor A only**; it does not assign Motor B encoder pins.

## Run the encoder test

Keep the step 7 wiring and upload [encoder_test](#/docs/Micromouse2026/Debug/encoder_test.md). Raise the wheel before uploading: the motor starts automatically after its startup pause at PWM 85.

Use 115200 baud and No Line Ending. Green means running; red means stopped. The sketch prints a count and ticks/s once per second. Send **s** to stop, **g** to go, or **z** to zero the count.

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Counts increase; ticks/s roughly steady while spinning | Encoder is reporting movement |
| Count settles; ticks/s becomes zero after s | Motor stopped |
| Zero while spinning | Check encoder 3V3/GND and C1 connection |
| Negative ticks/s | Power off and swap C1/C2 |
| Count jumps while stopped | Check noise from nearby motor wires |

## Other motor checks

Use [motor_sweep](#/docs/Micromouse2026/Debug/motor_sweep.md) if a motor needs more duty to start. Check a sagging battery or jammed wheel if it hums without turning.

**Unplug the motor before [pin_test](#/docs/Micromouse2026/Debug/pin_test.md).** It drives PWM fully HIGH and would apply the whole 9 V to the 6 V motor.
