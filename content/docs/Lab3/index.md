# Workshop 03 - Servos & Linkages

Bring together the RGB LEDs from Workshop 01 and the servos from Workshop 02. Guided stations cover the fundamentals, while confident builders can take on the more advanced mechanisms and integration challenges.

This lab will explore:
- **Driving RGB LEDs** with microcontrollers: PWM for smooth colour mixing and timed light effects
- More **advanced Servo mechanisms**: angle mapping, speed control, and chaining multiple servos
- **Mechanical linkages and robotic arms**: simple linkages, mounting servos, and basic kinematics
- **Interfacing an LCD screen** to show simple menus

By the end, participants will have a working prototype that combines light, motion, controls, and mechanical linkages. No prior experience is required.
