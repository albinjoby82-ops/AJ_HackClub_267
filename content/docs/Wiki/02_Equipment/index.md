# Electronics & Test Equipment

Learn what common laboratory tools do, how to connect them, and how to use them without damaging your circuit or the equipment.

Always check the relevant safety guidance before switching equipment on.

{: .warning-title}
> Start with the circuit unpowered
>
> Make connections and select instrument settings before enabling power or outputs. These guides cover supervised, low-voltage Hack Club work and do not authorise work on mains or other hazardous circuits.

## In this section

- [Bench Power Supply](02_01_PowerSupply.md) — provide controlled voltage with current limiting
- [Signal Generator](02_02_SignalGenerator.md) — produce repeatable test waveforms
- [Multimeter](02_03_Multimeter.md) — measure voltage, current, resistance, and continuity
- [Oscilloscope](02_04_Oscilloscope.md) — display voltage changing over time
- [Breadboards](02_05_Breadboards.md) — build and troubleshoot temporary circuits

## A useful test-bench order

1. Build and inspect the circuit with all outputs disabled.
2. Check continuity and resistance where appropriate.
3. Configure the power supply and current limit.
4. Enable power while monitoring current.
5. Apply test signals only after the circuit's supply rails are correct.
6. Measure the circuit response and disable outputs before rewiring.
