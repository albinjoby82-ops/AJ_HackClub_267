# Soldering, Heat and Fumes

Soldering combines a burn hazard, irritating fumes, chemicals and possible lead exposure. It is suitable for Build Club when the workspace, extraction and supervision are appropriate.

## Minimum controls

- Use a temperature-controlled iron in a stable stand.
- Use local fume extraction positioned to draw fumes away from the breathing zone.
- Wear eye protection.
- Tie back hair and keep cables clear of the iron.
- Never solder a powered circuit or battery.
- Identify whether the solder contains lead.
- Wash hands after soldering and before eating or drinking.
- Keep food and drinks out of the soldering area.

Do not rely on a small desk fan that merely blows fumes towards another person.

## Hot work habits

- Treat the iron as hot whenever it is plugged in.
- Do not test temperature with a finger.
- Return the iron to its stand between joints.
- Allow components, boards and desoldered solder to cool before handling.
- Direct clipped leads away from people and collect them.
- Switch off, clean and safely store the iron after use.

## If solder or flux contacts someone

For a burn, chemical exposure or material in the eye, stop work and get a trained first aider. Follow the product safety data sheet and local first-aid procedure. Do not apply improvised chemicals or ointments.
