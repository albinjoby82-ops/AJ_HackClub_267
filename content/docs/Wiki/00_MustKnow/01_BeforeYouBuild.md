# Before You Build

Use this check before starting a new activity or significantly changing one.

## The 60-second check

### People

- Does everyone know what the build is meant to do?
- Has anyone using a restricted tool completed the required induction?
- Can everyone participate and leave the area safely?
- Is someone trained available when the activity needs supervision?

### Place

- Keep exits, aisles, extinguishers, first-aid equipment and electrical isolation points accessible.
- Remove drinks from electronics and fabrication areas.
- Tie back long hair and secure loose clothing or jewellery near moving tools.
- Provide enough light, ventilation and bench space.
- Route cables so they do not become trip or snag hazards.

### Project

- What can become hot, sharp, fast, live, pressurised or chemically hazardous?
- What happens if software crashes, a wire comes loose or power returns unexpectedly?
- What is the worst credible mistake, and how will you limit its consequences?
- Can you lower the voltage, current, temperature, speed or stored energy?
- Is there a safe way to stop it quickly?

## A lightweight risk check

For ordinary club builds, write four short lines:

| Question | Example answer |
| --- | --- |
| What could cause harm? | Shorted LiPo, hot soldering iron, rotating propeller |
| Who could be affected? | Builder and people beside the bench |
| What controls will we use? | Current limit, iron stand, remove propeller |
| What would make us stop? | Swelling, smoke, damaged insulation, missing guard |

If the controls are unclear or depend entirely on “being careful,” pause and ask for help.

{: .tip-title}
> Use a pre-power check
>
> Confirm polarity, component orientation, supply voltage, current limit and exposed conductors. Ask another person to inspect an unfamiliar circuit before power is applied.

## Housekeeping during the build

- Return hot and sharp tools to their stands or storage places.
- Collect clipped leads; they can enter equipment or injure eyes.
- Keep component bags and waste out of moving machinery.
- Label projects left on benches with owner, date, supply requirements and hazards.
- Disconnect or make safe unattended projects unless a specific procedure permits them to run.
