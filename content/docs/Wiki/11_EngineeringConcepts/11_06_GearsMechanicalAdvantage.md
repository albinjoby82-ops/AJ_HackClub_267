# Gears and Mechanical Advantage

Gears trade rotational speed for torque and can change direction.

```text
gear ratio = driven gear teeth / driving gear teeth
```

A 10-tooth driving gear turning a 30-tooth driven gear gives a `3:1` reduction:

- Output speed is approximately one third.
- Ideal output torque is approximately three times larger.
- Direction reverses.

Real mechanisms lose energy through friction, deformation and poor alignment.

## Related mechanisms

- **Pulley and belt:** quiet, flexible spacing, can slip
- **Chain and sprocket:** positive drive over distance
- **Lead screw:** rotation to precise linear motion
- **Rack and pinion:** rotation to linear motion
- **Lever:** force-distance trade-off around a pivot

## Backlash

Clearance between parts causes lost motion when direction reverses. It matters in robots, printers and positioning systems.

Consider speed, torque, alignment, bearing loads, material strength, backlash, wear and guarding.
