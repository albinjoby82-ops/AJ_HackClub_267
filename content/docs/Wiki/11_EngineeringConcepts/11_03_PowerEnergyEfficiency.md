# Power, Energy and Efficiency

**Power** is the rate at which energy is transferred.

```text
P = V × I
```

A device using `5 V` at `0.4 A` consumes `2 W`.

## Energy over time

```text
energy = power × time
```

Battery energy is often stated in watt-hours:

```text
Wh ≈ voltage × amp-hours
```

A nominal `3.7 V`, `2 Ah` cell contains roughly `7.4 Wh`, but usable energy depends on load, converter losses, temperature, ageing and safe voltage limits.

## Heat and efficiency

For a resistor:

```text
P = I²R
```

Doubling current produces four times the resistive heating.

```text
efficiency = useful output power / input power
```

No real system is 100% efficient. Ask where lost energy becomes heat and whether the device can remove it safely.
