Built for Hack Club events
"Learn by making something real."

Build Hub turns practical electronics material into reusable sessions for Hack Club organizers, mentors, and hackers. Pick a workshop, gather the listed parts, and teach it at a hackathon, club meeting, or community event.

  • microcontrollers to motors,
  • soldering to 3D printing,
  • programming to digital design, and more!

Each track is beginner-friendly, hands-on, and designed to help participants leave with a working build and the confidence to keep experimenting.
