## Working rules

Shared facts live in [EVENT_FACTS.md](EVENT_FACTS.md) and nowhere else. Unconfirmed
details are marked `[DECISION REQUIRED: ...]` or `[INFO REQUIRED: ...]` in place and
collected in the decision queue. Nothing about the event is invented.

Published under `hackclub.ucdelecsoc.com/micromouse`. See the publishing notes in
[DOCS_GUIDE.md](DOCS_GUIDE.md).
