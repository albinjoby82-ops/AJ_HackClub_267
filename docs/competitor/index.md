## Quick answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Do I need robotics experience? | No. Complete beginners are who this is built for |
| Do I bring a robot? | No. Every team gets an identical kit on the day |
| Do I bring anything? | A laptop, a charger and a USB data cable. [Full list](08-venue-and-what-to-bring.md#bring) |
| Can I use AI coding agents? | Yes, and you are encouraged to. [Policy](04-ai-agent-policy.md) |
| How long is the build? | Six hours |
| What wins? | The fastest verified run to the maze centre, plus design and reliability awards |
| Who do I ask? | eleceng@ucdsocieties.ie |
