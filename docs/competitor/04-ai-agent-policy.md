# AI coding agent policy

| | |
|---|---|
| **Allowed** | Yes. Use any AI coding agent you like, all day, on your own machine. |
| **Encouraged** | Yes. Six hours is short. Agents are part of how we expect teams to work. |
| **Your responsibility** | The code is yours. You own what it does, and you must be able to explain it. |

## What this means

Use an AI coding agent for firmware, maze solving, debugging, wiring notes,
anything. There is no separate "no AI" category and no scoring penalty.

Sponsor credits may be supplied for agent use. See
[A7 Sponsor credits](07-sponsor-credits.md) for what is offered and
how to get it.

## Explaining your code

A judge or demonstrator may ask how part of your micromouse works: your wall
following, your search, your motor control. Answer in your own words. You do
not need to have typed every line, but you do need to understand it. A team
that cannot explain its own code may lose credit in the design award.

Award criteria are in [A3 Rules and scoring](03-rules-and-scoring.md).

## Shared resources versus your work

Anything published by the organisers, such as the preparation video series, the
written setup documentation and any starter code, is shared. Every team may use
it and it is not judged as your work.

Everything you add on top of that is your team's work, whether you typed it or
an agent did. Do not copy another team's code.

Ask at eleceng@ucdsocieties.ie if you are unsure whether something counts.
