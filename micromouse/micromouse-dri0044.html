H2 · Motor driver

DFRobot DRI0044

Drives both wheel motors. Each motor needs one direction signal and one PWM speed signal.

01 Ratings and the one rule
Channels2 brushed DC motors, one per wheel
Continuous current1.2 A per channel
Logic supply VCC2.7–5.5 V
Motor supply VM2.5–12 V

Keep VCC and VM separate. VCC is logic, VM is motor power. The GA12-N20 motors are rated 6 V, so check H6 before connecting VM to a full 2S pack.

02 Wiring
VCCESP32 3V3
GNDESP32 ground and motor-supply ground
DIR1 / PWM1Two ESP32 outputs: left direction / speed
DIR2 / PWM2Two ESP32 outputs: right direction / speed
VMMotor supply
M1+ M1- / M2+ M2-Left motor / right motor

Take the four GPIO numbers from your ESP32-C6 pin map.

03 Test before mounting
  1. Wheels off the table. Check battery polarity, VCC, VM and the shared ground.
  2. Start one motor at about 20–25% PWM.
  3. Test forward, stop and reverse, then repeat for the second motor.
  4. Stop if the ESP32 resets, the driver heats quickly or the current is unexpected.

If a wheel spins the wrong way, swap that motor's two output wires or invert its direction in software.
