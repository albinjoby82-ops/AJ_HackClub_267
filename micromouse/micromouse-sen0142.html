H3 · Motion sensor

DFRobot SEN0142 IMU

A 6-axis accelerometer and gyro. On the mouse its job is turn angle: the gyro tells you how far you have rotated.

01 What it gives you
SensorMPU-6050 (3-axis accelerometer + 3-axis gyro)
InterfaceI2C, address 0x68
Supply3–5 V. Use the ESP32 3V3 rail.

The gyro drifts and there is no compass. Use it for heading over a run, and correct it with encoders and wall sensors. See S4 Motion and hardware.

02 Wiring
VIN / GNDESP32 3V3 / GND
SDA / SCLThe shared I2C bus, alongside the distance sensors
INTOptional data-ready input
AUX_DA, AUX_CLLeave unconnected
03 Orientation and calibration
  1. Fix the board flat and note which edge points forward.
  2. Run an I2C scan and look for 0x68.
  3. Keep the mouse completely still at power-up and average the gyro readings for a few seconds. That is your bias.
  4. Rotate one axis at a time and confirm a left turn gives the sign your code expects.

A rotated board or an uncorrected bias gives the wrong turn direction or a heading that walks off. Never calibrate while the bench is being nudged.
