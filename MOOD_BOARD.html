03 · Circuit motifs

PCB, not sci-fi

The electronics identity comes from quiet drafting details — traces, pin headers, and silkscreen labels — supporting the Hack Club palette without competing with it.

Trace dividers
Section breaks drawn as PCB traces with a solder-dot terminal. Stroke at ~16% opacity.

06 · Rules of thumb

Keep it on the rails

Whitespace is the layout

Hairline borders and air, not boxes and fills. If a section feels busy, remove a border before adding one.

One orange per view

Orange marks the single most urgent thing — a deadline, a live event. Two oranges means neither matters.

Traces stay quiet

Circuit motifs at 10–18% opacity, decorative only. If a trace competes with content, it's too loud.

Mono means metadata

Monospace is reserved for labels, counts, timestamps and chips — never body copy or headings.
